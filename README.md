# Food-Delivery-Data-Visualization-Portfolio-

This project presents a complete Food Delivery Business Performance Data Visualization Portfolio using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.
The objective is to explore the dataset visually, identify meaningful patterns, compare business performance, and communicate insights through appropriate charts.

The analysis covers trends in orders and revenue, differences between cities and cuisines, relationships between marketing spending and revenue, delivery time and customer ratings, and the effect of weather and order channels on business performance.

**Objectives**
The main objectives of this project are:

Understand the structure of the Food Delivery dataset.
Perform basic data inspection before visualization.
Analyze numerical and categorical variables.
Study the distribution of important business variables.
Analyze changes in orders and revenue over time.
Compare performance across cities.
Compare order volume across cuisines.
Study the relationship between marketing spending and revenue.
Analyze the relationship between delivery time and customer ratings.
Examine the effect of weather conditions on performance.
Compare different order channels.
Identify correlations between numerical variables.
Communicate meaningful business insights using visualizations.

**Interpretation Approach**
Each visualization is created with a specific analytical purpose.

The charts are not treated as standalone graphics. Each visualization is followed by a short interpretation explaining the pattern observed and its possible business meaning.
